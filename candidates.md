# Candidates — One Emoji, One Dream Vacation

Board context (781 entries): the board is dominated by 🏝️ desert island,
🗻 Mount Fuji, 🌅 sunrise, whales, Moai, hot-air balloons, the Milky Way,
and canoes. All of those are deliberately excluded below — the winner must
be a fresh angle that is still unmistakably a dream vacation.

Judge criteria, in order (no ties in any head-to-head):
1. Exactly one emoji, unmistakably a dream vacation (beach, mountain, plane, hammock, somewhere far away).
2. Evokes travel at a glance, like a postcard.
3. Feels personal and specific, not a generic vacation.
4. A spark of cleverness / surprise — a fresh choice.
5. Instantly legible across cultures and screens, clear at small size, not too vague.

## Winner

**🛖 Hut — 5/5, unbeaten.**

It wins every pair, and for the right reasons: on criterion 1 it is
unmistakably a dream stay (not an animal, a drink, or a hazard); on
criterion 2 it is the whole postcard in one glyph; on criterion 3 it is
personal and specific — *your* hut on a far-away island, not a generic
beach; on criterion 4 it is fresh (the board is full of islands, Fuji,
sunrises and whales, not huts); on criterion 5 its thatched-roof
silhouette stays legible at small size across cultures. The runner-up
🦩 flamingo is vivid but can read as a zoo animal, which is exactly the
ambiguity the hut avoids.
